Privacy Policy · Effective 25 September 2026

Your numbers stay on your phone.

Bilans is a finance calculator for Android — pay, loans, savings, exchange rates and invoices. It is built so that the developer never receives your data.

IThe short version

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting services.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • The developer does not receive, see or store any of your data.

IIWhat is stored on your device

Bilans keeps the following in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your settings (language, country, appearance);
  • saved and recent calculations;
  • invoices, clients and the business details you enter (name, address, tax ID, bank account);
  • your team list (names, roles and pay), if you use Team cost;
  • downloaded exchange rates, so the converter works offline;
  • your Bilans Pro status.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Delete all data, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

IIIInternet connections

Bilans connects to the internet only to:

  • download official exchange rates — National Bank of Serbia rates through kurs.resenje.org and European Central Bank reference rates through Frankfurter. These requests name only the currencies and dates needed, never your amounts or other data. As with any internet request, those services see your device's IP address; they operate under their own policies;
  • reach Google Play, for purchases and to open the app's store page.

IVSharing, exports and backups

When you share a result, a PDF or an invoice, or export a backup, Android's share sheet sends the file to the app or place you choose, and that recipient's terms apply from then on. Exported backups are ordinary files — keep them somewhere safe.

Android may also include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, according to your device's backup settings.

VPurchases

Bilans Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. The app receives from Google Play only the status of your purchase — which product and whether it is active — and keeps it on your device to unlock Pro. Google's Privacy Policy applies to payments.

VIChildren

Bilans is made for adults and is not directed at children.

VIIYour rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or erase on our side: all of it is under your control on your device, in line with the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection and the EU GDPR.

VIIIChanges and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Questions: write to the address under Developer contact on Bilans' Google Play page.

Politika privatnosti · Važi od 25. septembra 2026.

Vaši brojevi ostaju na vašem telefonu.

Bilans je finansijski kalkulator za Android — plata, krediti, štednja, kursevi i fakture. Napravljen je tako da programer nikada ne dobija vaše podatke.

IUkratko

  • Nema naloga ni prijavljivanja.
  • Nema reklama, analitike, praćenja ni servisa za prijavu grešaka.
  • Sve što unesete čuva se samo na vašem uređaju.
  • Programer ne prima, ne vidi i ne čuva vaše podatke.

IIŠta se čuva na uređaju

Bilans u privatnom prostoru aplikacije na telefonu čuva:

  • podešavanja (jezik, zemlja, izgled);
  • sačuvane i nedavne obračune;
  • fakture, klijente i podatke o firmi koje unesete (naziv, adresa, PIB, račun);
  • spisak zaposlenih (imena, pozicije i plate), ako koristite Trošak tima;
  • preuzete kurseve, da bi konvertor radio i bez interneta;
  • status paketa Bilans Pro.

Sve možete obrisati u bilo kom trenutku: Podešavanja → Obriši sve podatke, brisanjem podataka aplikacije u podešavanjima Androida ili deinstalacijom.

IIIInternet veze

Bilans se povezuje na internet samo da bi:

  • preuzeo zvanične kurseve — kurs Narodne banke Srbije preko kurs.resenje.org i referentne kurseve Evropske centralne banke preko Frankfurter. Ti zahtevi sadrže samo valute i datume, nikada vaše iznose ni druge podatke. Kao i kod svakog zahteva na internetu, ti servisi vide IP adresu uređaja i posluju po sopstvenim pravilima;
  • pristupio Google Play-u, radi kupovine i otvaranja stranice aplikacije.

IVDeljenje, izvoz i rezervne kopije

Kada podelite rezultat, PDF ili fakturu, ili izvezete rezervnu kopiju, Android šalje fajl aplikaciji ili mestu koje sami izaberete, i od tada važe njihova pravila. Rezervne kopije su obični fajlovi — čuvajte ih na sigurnom.

Android može uključiti podatke aplikacije u rezervnu kopiju uređaja na vašem Google nalogu, u skladu sa podešavanjima uređaja.

VKupovina

Bilans Pro se prodaje preko Google Play-a. Plaćanje obrađuje Google; programer nikada ne vidi podatke o plaćanju. Aplikacija od Google Play-a dobija samo status kupovine — koji paket i da li je aktivan — i čuva ga na uređaju. Za plaćanje važi Google-ova politika privatnosti.

VIDeca

Bilans je namenjen odraslima i nije usmeren na decu.

VIIVaša prava

Pošto programer ne raspolaže vašim podacima o ličnosti, kod nas nema šta da se uvidi, ispravi ili obriše: sve je pod vašom kontrolom na vašem uređaju, u skladu sa Zakonom o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti i GDPR-om.

VIIIIzmene i kontakt

Ako se politika promeni, nova verzija biće objavljena na ovoj adresi sa novim datumom. Pitanja: pišite na adresu navedenu pod Kontakt programera na Google Play stranici aplikacije Bilans.
