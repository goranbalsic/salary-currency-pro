## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Salary & Currency Pro is a personal-finance toolkit built for Serbia,
Croatia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Slovenia,
Bulgaria, Albania, and Romania — in your own language, on your own
device, with no account required.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Salary calculators for all 9 countries — gross-to-net and net-to-gross,
  using each country's real tax brackets, contributions, and allowances,
  sourced and dated so you know exactly which year's rules you're using.
• Currency converter with live exchange rates that falls back to the
  last known rate when you're offline.
• VAT calculator with each country's standard rate, editable for reduced
  rates.
• Loan and savings calculators, a 50/30/20 budget planner, and a
  freelancer payout reality-check tool (platform fees, bank fees, and
  real conversion together).
• Expense and income tracker with monthly summaries, category budgets,
  and savings goals.
• A simple invoice tracker for freelancers and small businesses — client,
  amount, due date, paid/unpaid status.
• Self-assessment tax calculator for freelancers in your country —
  income tax and social contributions on self-employed income, using
  local rules and the local filing form.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
Every payroll and tax figure is sourced from official or professional
tax-advisory references and dated, not guessed — and each calculator
tells you exactly which year and effective date its numbers are based
on. This app has no account, no cloud sync, and no server: everything
you enter stays on your device. Currency conversion is the only feature
that needs an internet connection; without one, the last known rate is
used instead.

Available in 9 languages: Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian, Macedonian,
Slovenian, Bulgarian, Albanian, Romanian, and English.

Free to use, with optional ads. A Pro subscription removes ads.

This app provides estimates only, not professional tax or financial
advice.
