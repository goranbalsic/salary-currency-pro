### 11.1 — Paušal turnover tracker

New `lib/models/pausal_turnover.dart` (`PausalThresholdState`,
`PausalLimitStatus`, `PausalInvoiceContribution`, `PausalTurnoverSummary`)
and `lib/services/pausal_tracker_service.dart`. Fed entirely by the
existing `InvoiceService` — no new turnover data-entry surface. Tracks
the calendar-year paušal ceiling and the rolling-365-day VAT threshold
simultaneously, both threshold values read live from `tax_rules.json`'s
`rs` regime rather than duplicated as literals. 70/85/95%/exceeded states,
always paired with a text label. A simple current-pace linear projection
for the paušal ceiling, shown only with ≥14 days of data and a positive,
plausible pace.

**Rate-history design decision:** since there's no historical FX archive,
the first time a foreign-currency invoice is seen, its RSD rate is
captured once (live fetch, falling back to cache) and stored permanently
against that invoice id — immutable afterward, so totals stay stable
across repeat views. Labeled honestly in the tap-through breakdown as
"rate: {source}", never claimed as an authoritative invoice-date rate. An
invoice whose rate could never be captured stays excluded and counted,
and self-heals (gets included) the next time a live rate succeeds.

New `lib/screens/tools/pausal_tracker_screen.dart` — two limit cards with
progress bars, an excluded-invoice banner with tap-through, a "See the
numbers" breakdown sheet listing every included/excluded invoice with its
rate and source, and an "assessed monthly paušal amount" input (the user's
own figure from their tax ruling — the app never computes this) with a
45.05% decomposition computed from four sourced fields (added a fourth,
`pausalTaxRatePercentOfDeemedBase`, to `tax_rules.json` for this — the
other three already existed). Registered in the Tools hub
(`HistoryToolIds.pausalTracker`).

### 11.2 — Monthly obligation reminder extension

`NotificationService.setPausalReminderEnabled` gained an optional,
additive lead reminder (`pausalLeadReminderId`, day 12 — 3 days before
the existing day-15 one), gated behind its own preference and requiring
the caller to supply distinct lead title/body text, same "caller owns all
user-facing strings" pattern as every other reminder. Settings screen
gained a conditional second switch (visible only when the main reminder
is on) and now builds the notification body dynamically, including the
stored assessed amount when set (`notifPausalReminderNotifBodyWithAmount`).

### 11.3 — Model A vs Model B quarterly comparator

New `_RsComparatorSection` in `freelance_tax_screen.dart`, deliberately
independent of the existing single-model result above it — always
computes and shows both models side by side for whatever income is
currently typed. Added an "already insured elsewhere" toggle (wired
through the same `insuredElsewhere` option the strategy fix introduced),
a quarter selector with a filing deadline computed from the sourced
`filingDeadlineDaysAfterQuarterEnd` field, the min-PIO-base note when it
actually binds, and the recommended model with its net-income delta.
"Formula breakdown on tap" reuses the calculator's existing row-by-row
breakdown rather than inventing a second mechanism — a deliberate,
disclosed scope choice.
